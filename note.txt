# BUG - Header Navigation Off by One Field

The latest test identified the first incorrect field.

Current behavior:

1. Invoice Date ✅ Correct
2. Cr Credit ✅ Correct
3. P Purc. ✅ Correct
4. SR No: P ✅ Correct
5. Received ❌ Incorrect

The "Received" field is already pre-filled with its default value.

The automation is typing the Supplier name into the "Received" field instead of the Supplier field.

As a result:

Received  ← Supplier name (wrong)
Supplier  ← Invoice Number
Supplier Bill No ← Supplier Bill Date
...

Every remaining field shifts one field earlier.

==================================================

Required fix:

After reaching the "Received" field:

DO NOT type anything.

Simply press Enter once to move to the Supplier field.

Then continue:

Supplier
→ invoice.supplier
Enter

Supplier Bill No
→ invoice.invoice_number
Enter

Supplier Bill Date
→ today's date
Enter

==================================================

Do not change any other navigation.

Only correct the transition from "Received" to "Supplier".

==================================================

Deliverables:

- Files modified
- Exact change made
- Confirmation that:
  - Received remains unchanged.
  - Supplier is entered in the Supplier field.
  - Supplier Bill No is entered in the correct field.
  - Supplier Bill Date is entered in the correct field.
